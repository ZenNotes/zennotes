Atlas

A stable map of the vault, floating in space. Regions are constellations of linked notes; node size is how linked a note is; the camera orbits, and the map drifts gently when your hands are off the keys. Positions are computed once and cached, so the sky looks the same tomorrow. Lenses recolor it to answer a question.

f hint jump to any visible note
/ filter by title or tag
v toggle the sky (3D) and the map (2D)
h j k l orbit and tilt in 3D, pan in 2D
+ / - fly closer / further
Enter open the focused note
o rings around the focused note
z z center on the focused note
[ / ] previous / next region
m mark twice, trace the path
c links: quiet, all, off
t replay the vault through time
a accept best suggested link
Esc unwind one layer

Lenses

1 Structure: the plain sky
2 Heat: glow is recent editing
3 Orphans: notes with no links
4 Bridges: notes joining regions
5 Suggestions: ghost links to accept

In the real app

Opens with Space g or :atlas. Mouse: drag orbits, shift-drag pans, wheel flies. With vim mode off, arrows orbit, Enter opens, Escape unwinds, and the toolbar carries everything else.
